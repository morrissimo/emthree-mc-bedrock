# Explosives & Gadgets

A mix of thrown explosives and handy standalone gadgets.

## Explosives

All three are **thrown** (right-click to lob) and **detonate the instant they hit** something — no lit-fuse delay — and none of them start fires. All craft **8 at a time** at a vanilla crafting table.

| Explosive | Blast power | Breaks blocks? | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grenade | 1 | **No** — entities only | Safe to use near your base |
| Bomb | 2 | Yes\* | A bit weaker than a creeper (3) |
| Dynamite | 4 | Yes\* | TNT-equivalent blast |

\* Block-breaking respects the world's `mobGriefing` rule — turn it off and even Bomb/Dynamite won't damage terrain.

## Accelerator Clock

The activator for the [Pedestal](utility_blocks.md#pedestal) crop accelerator. On its own it does nothing — seat it on a Pedestal and that pedestal starts fast-growing crops in a 9×9 area. Think of it as the pedestal's "battery."

## Fiber

An early-game **string substitute**. You get it by breaking **leaves with a [Flint Knife](tools.md#flint-knife)** (1–2 fiber per leaf block, ~50%). Combine **4 fiber → 1 string** at a vanilla table — handy before you've found sheep or spiders. It's a drop, not a crafted item.

## Mortar

A **reusable crafting tool** — it's used as an ingredient in a few recipes (e.g. brewing a plain water bottle) and **returned intact** afterward, so a single mortar lasts forever.
