# Utility Blocks

A grab-bag of standalone quality-of-life blocks — lighting, water, roads, farm automation. None of them need UC power.

## Pedestal

A **crop-growth accelerator** (not a display stand). Right-click it holding an [Accelerator Clock](explosives.md#accelerator-clock) to seat the clock on it; once loaded, it repeatedly nudges crops to grow in a **9×9 area** around it (radius 4) — 27 growth attempts per cycle at an 80% base chance (scaled down for higher-tier UC crops). Right-click again to take the clock back. Works on UC crops, vanilla crops, and modded crops.

## Lantern

An even **bigger light** — it projects the Big Torch's lighting pattern from four points, covering roughly **±33 blocks** from center at light 15. The go-to for lighting a whole base or cavern from one block. Unlike the Big Torch it's a solid block.

Same idea as the Drill Placer, but deploys a rideable **Tractor** — an **auto-harvester**. Drive it across a field and it reaps mature crops in a **3×3 swath**, pulls in nearby dropped items, and stores the yield in its inventory. ## Asphalt

A **speed road**. Walking on it grants **Speed II** (refreshed as you keep moving), so paved paths make travel much faster. Crafts **8 at a time**. Bonus: right-click it with a block to **camouflage** its look (asphalt / grass path / grass / dirt / cobblestone / stone / gravel).
