# Deepslate Tools

Adds a full deepslate-tier tool set. The standout features are the **hammer** (3×3 AOE mining for stone & ore) and the **excavator** (3×3 AOE digging for dirt, sand & gravel) — each breaks an entire face of blocks at once.

## Tools

All crafted at a vanilla crafting table using standard tool patterns with **polished_deepslate** + sticks. Recipe unlock: having **cobbled_deepslate** in inventory.

All tools are enchantable and repair with polished_deepslate (1 deepslate restores ~1/6 max durability).

## Hammer — 3×3 AOE Mining

When the hammer breaks a "hammerable" block, it also breaks the **8 surrounding blocks** in the same plane (the full 3×3 face) — all with their normal drops.

**Hammerable blocks include:** stone, cobblestone, deepslate variants, ores, bricks, ice, packed ice, and most other stone-type blocks.

> A full 3×3 swing costs about **5 durability** (the center block plus its neighbors, charged at a discount). Unbreaking stretches that a lot further — see [Enchantments](#enchantments). Keep a repair supply of polished deepslate handy.

## Excavator — 3×3 AOE Digging

The excavator is the hammer's earth-moving cousin: breaking a "shoveable" block clears the **full 3×3 face** of shoveable blocks at once — ideal for digging out sand pits, gravel, and foundations.

**Shoveable blocks include:** dirt, grass, sand, gravel, clay, snow, soul sand/soil, mycelium, podzol, concrete powder, and similar.

It digs **deliberately slowly** — each block is slower than a normal shovel. The wide area is the payoff, not raw speed. A full 3×3 swing costs about **5 durability**, the same discounted model as the hammer.

## Enchantments

All deepslate tools accept their usual enchantments from an enchanting table or anvil. A few notes specific to this set:

- **Efficiency, Unbreaking, Mending** — all work as expected. Unbreaking genuinely stretches durability here, so it's especially worth putting on the hammer and excavator.
- **Fortune / Silk Touch on the hammer & excavator** — only the **block you actually aim at** benefits. The 8 surrounding blocks cleared by the AOE drop their normal loot (no Fortune/Silk). Consider it the fair price of breaking nine blocks at once — for a max-Fortune yield on a specific ore, mine that block directly.
- **Sword enchantments** (Sharpness, Looting, etc.) — fully standard.

## Hammer Debuff

When the hammer-holder takes a hit, they receive:
- **Mining Fatigue V** for 5 seconds
- **Weakness III** for 5 seconds

Flavor for the hammer's weight — swap to a sword before combat.
